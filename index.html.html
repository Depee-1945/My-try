What Is the Solar System?

The solar system is made up of the Sun and all the objects that move around it. These objects include eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets. The Sun is at the centre of our solar system and provides the light and heat that make life on Earth possible.

The Eight Planets

The eight planets travel around the Sun in their own orbits. They are:

  • Mercury – the closest planet to the Sun.
  • Venus – the hottest planet in the solar system.
  • Earth – the planet where we live.
  • Mars – known as the Red Planet.
  • Jupiter – the largest planet in the solar system.
  • Saturn – famous for its beautiful rings.
  • Uranus – a very cold planet that rotates on its side.
  • Neptune – a distant, cold and windy planet.
